IN ORDER TO BECOME THE VALUE ADDER FOR WALMART

I don't just apply to Walmart. I wrote a practitioner manual on landed-cost drivers.

When a customer pays for a product, they pay the landed cost - not just the factory price. I authored Global Logistics: From Sourcing to Shipping, a practitioner manual on landed-cost reduction, built from visits to 16 manufacturing sites across 4 Asian markets. It breaks landed cost into its real drivers, and I applied the framework to a simulated $10,000+ cross-border transaction to evaluate them. Getting landed cost right is how a team protects the customer: lower cost, fewer surprises, faster and more reliable delivery.

Tap a driver above. Freight: the cost of moving the product by air, sea, rail, or road - the biggest swing in what a customer finally pays, and the first place a good plan saves money.
